Profile

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from North South University (2024), specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision and applied research — most recently, building multimodal systems that fuse visual and motion signals to recognize Bangla Sign Language in real time.

Published Work

Bangla Sign Language Recognition with Multimodal Deep Learning Fusion

A. HASAN · M. H. JOBAYER · M. A. A. M. PIAS · T. ALAM · R. KHAN — ENGINEERING REPORTS, VOL. 7, NO. 4, 2025

We developed a multimodal Bangla Sign Language recognition system that fuses visual keypoints with audio features, training ViT, VGGish, and CNN-LSTM models and combining their predictions through late fusion. The approach reached 94.71% accuracy, and the project contributed a new dataset spanning 200 word classes — a step toward more accessible communication tools for the deaf and hard-of-hearing community in Bangladesh.
