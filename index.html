I’m a Computer Science & Engineering graduate of North South University (2024), specialising in AI, machine learning and deep learning. My thesis built multimodal systems that fuse visual and audio signals to recognise Bangla Sign Language.

It was published in Engineering Reports (Wiley): ViT, VGGish and CNN-LSTM models combined by late fusion reached 94.71% accuracy on a 200-word-class dataset we helped create from scratch.

I’ve also shipped full-stack academic systems, studied malware detection through static analysis, and led volunteer relief work for flood-affected communities in Bangladesh. I’m looking for entry-level roles in AI/ML engineering, applied research or software development.

Published research

Peer-reviewed work toward communication tools for the deaf and hard-of-hearing community in Bangladesh.

Bangla Sign Language Recognition with Multimodal Deep Learning Fusion

A. Hasan, M. H. Jobayer, M. A. A. M. Pias, T. Alam, R. Khan. Engineering Reports, vol. 7, no. 4, 2025

We fused visual keypoints with audio features, trained ViT, VGGish and CNN-LSTM models, and combined their predictions through late fusion to reach 94.71% accuracy. The project also contributed a new dataset of 200 word classes.
